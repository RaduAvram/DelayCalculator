📐 Measurement Manual & Field Guide

1. Subwoofer to Listening Point (D_sub-L & D_sub-R) Dsub (m)
0 cm Start
Place 0 cm tape tip at the exact acoustic center of the subwoofer front baffle:
• Dual-driver subwoofers (2×18"): Position the tape end dead-center on the front grille, halfway between the centers of the top and bottom drivers.
• Single-driver subwoofers (1×18" / 1×15"): Position the tape end directly over the center of the driver's dust cap.
Pull Tape
Pull the measuring tape in a straight direct line of sight (line-of-propagation) from the subwoofer baffle to the designated listening position (FOH mix console or RTA measurement mic). Keep the tape taut without sagging.
Target End
Read the distance at ear height (~1.6 m seated / ~1.7 m standing) or directly at the tip of the RTA measurement microphone diaphragm.
💡 Pro Sound Tip — L/R Symmetry: Always measure Sub Left and Sub Right independently. Even a 34 cm distance delta produces a 1.0 ms arrival time difference that must be compensated in Step 1 (Sub ↔ Sub Sync) on the closer subwoofer.
2. Top-to-Sub Vertical Height Offset (V) V (m) · Δ Height
0 cm Start
Place 0 cm tape tip at the horizontal centerline / acoustic center of the Subwoofer:
Align the tape with the center line of the subwoofer's front baffle.
Plumb Line
Extend the tape vertically straight up (90° perpendicular to the floor/stage deck) along the speaker mounting pole or rigging support line.
Target End
Read the height at the acoustic center of the Top cabinet:
Typically located at the vertical midpoint between the high-frequency horn throat and the mid-bass woofer cone.
📐 Why Height (V) Matters Acoustically: Because the Top is elevated, its sound travels along the hypotenuse Dhyp = √(Dsub² + V²). This geometric path difference adds physical propagation delay to the Top cabinet that DelCalc automatically factors into the crossover alignment.
3. Voice Coil Depth Offset Offset (m) · + / -
Step A: Baffle Setback
Measure physical horizontal setback between front grilles:
Drop a plumb line or hold a straight edge down from the Top front grille to the Sub front grille. Measure the horizontal distance between the two planes.
Step B: Voice Coil Inset
Account for internal driver acoustic center depth:
• 18" Subwoofers: Deep cone places the voice coil ~8–15 cm behind the grille.
• 10"/12" Tops: Shallower cone places the voice coil ~4–8 cm behind the grille.
• Flush Grilles: If grilles are physically flush, the default offset is typically around +0.05 m to +0.08 m (Top voice coil sits physically in front of Sub voice coil).
⚙️ Depth Offset Rule of Thumb: For standard pole-mounted tops with grilles aligned flush with the sub front, use +0.08 m. If the top is pushed further back on the sub top plate by 10 cm, enter +0.18 m.
4. Stage Backline Alignment Distance Input Delay · Dbackline
0 cm Start
Place 0 cm tape tip at the unamplified acoustic sound source on stage:
Usually the center of the acoustic snare drum or the front grille of the loudest guitar/bass amplifier on stage.
Pull Tape
Extend tape straight forward downstage along the center line to the front baffle plane of the main PA speakers (Stage Deck Front line).
Master Input Delay
Apply this value to the System Input Delay (or Main Console Master Delay):
Delaying the entire PA allows the direct acoustic sound of the drums to reach the PA line exactly as the electronic PA signal launches, delivering a punchy, unified transient.
5. Professional Audio Terminology & Best Practices Field Engineering
Acoustic Center
The virtual spatial origin from which the acoustic wavefront appears to radiate (at or near the voice coil gap plane).
Plumb Line (Fir cu Plumb)
A strictly vertical line (90° to earth/stage) used to reference depth setback and vertical height between elevated tops and ground subs.
Hypotenuse Path (D_hyp)
The direct diagonal acoustic sound path from an elevated top speaker to audience ear height: Dhyp = √(D² + V²).
Group Delay (GD)
The frequency-dependent time delay introduced by crossover filter slopes and vented loudspeaker enclosures (especially sub HPF filters).
Crossover Summation Zone
The frequency band around fxo where sub and top contribute equal acoustic energy. Phase alignment ensures +6 dB constructive summation.
Laser Distance Measurer
When using a laser meter, hold the base against the cabinet grille center and shoot directly at the RTA microphone clipboard or FOH console hood.
🌡️ Speed of Sound Tip: Remember that temperature shifts change the speed of sound (c = 331.3 + 0.606 × T). Always check and update the Ambient Temperature field for daytime vs nighttime outdoor festivals.
